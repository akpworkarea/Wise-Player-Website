Support

Need help?

Pick what you are seeing.

My playlist will not load

Check that the playlist link is still active and typed correctly. For Xtream Codes, confirm the server URL, username and password with your provider.

Playback keeps stopping

Make sure your internet connection is stable. You can also raise the buffer size in Settings → Player.

A channel works for others but not for me

Some streams are limited by region and may need a VPN.

Wise Player does not host any content. Use only playlists you are allowed to watch.
