Privacy Policy

Effective Date: June 8, 2026

Wise Player ("we", "our", or "us") operates the Wise Player mobile application (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our App. By using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information:

  • Virtual Device Identifier: We generate a unique virtual device identifier derived from non-sensitive device information. This identifier is stored on our servers and associated with your device activation record for device authentication, license verification, fraud prevention, and authorized access to the App. We do not collect or store your device's actual hardware MAC address.
  • Playlist Data: When you upload or link an M3U/IPTV playlist URL, the URL may be stored in encrypted form on our servers solely for reference, synchronization, and streaming functionality. We do not analyze, modify, or share the content of your playlists.
  • Locally Stored Preferences: We store language preferences, favorite channels, playback settings, and selected playlists locally on your device using secure local storage. This information remains on your device unless you choose to remove it.
  • Usage Data: We may collect anonymized usage statistics, diagnostic information, crash reports, and feature usage information to improve app performance, reliability, and user experience. This information does not directly identify you.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To activate, authenticate, and verify authorized devices.
  • To provide, operate, and maintain the App.
  • To enable streaming and playlist synchronization features.
  • To improve performance, reliability, and user experience.
  • To diagnose technical issues and prevent misuse.
  • To comply with legal obligations when required.

3. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties.

Information may only be disclosed in the following circumstances:

  • Legal Requirements: When required by applicable law, court order, legal process, or governmental request.
  • Service Providers: To trusted service providers who assist us in operating, maintaining, or improving the App under confidentiality obligations.
  • Safety and Security: To protect the rights, property, security, or safety of our users, our services, or the public.

4. Data Security

We implement reasonable industry-standard security measures to protect your data, including encrypted storage of sensitive information and secure HTTPS communication for all supported network requests.

While we strive to protect your information, no method of electronic storage or transmission over the Internet can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

5. Data Retention

Virtual device identifiers and activation-related records are retained for as long as necessary to provide services, maintain licensing records, and comply with legal obligations.

Encrypted playlist URLs may be retained while your account or device activation remains active.

Locally stored preferences remain on your device until you clear app data or uninstall the App.

6. Data Deletion

You may request deletion of your account-related data, activation records, and stored identifiers by contacting us using the contact information below.

Valid deletion requests are typically processed within 30 days unless additional retention is required by law or for legitimate operational purposes.

Data stored locally on your device can be removed at any time by clearing application data or uninstalling the App.
